Services

What we can do for your film or show.

Whether you are preparing a feature, a series or a special, we can shape the campaign around it. Tell us what you need — we will meet you there.

Film marketing campaigns

From the first stills to opening week, we shape a campaign that feels true to the work and reaches the people who will love it.

Publicity, PR and press

Interviews, press notes, photocalls and careful media outreach — so the story around the film is as considered as the film itself.

Release and premiere strategy

Timing, premiere plans and a clear path to release, worked out with you rather than dropped on you.

Social media and trailer rollout

Trailers, clips and socials planned as a sequence, so each piece earns the next and the film arrives with momentum.

Festival and awards campaigns

A thoughtful presence at festivals and during awards season, when the right moment and the right room matter.

How we work

A clear path, from brief to release.

No mystery, no rush. Four simple stages, so you always know where we are and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Share the film or show, the dates you are working to, and what you need help with.

  2. 02

    Consult

    We watch, we listen, and we come back with a clear plan you can stand behind.

  3. 03

    Campaign

    Then we get to work: press, trailers, socials and appearances, timed to land.

  4. 04

    Release

    We stay close through premiere week and the weeks that follow, as the film meets its audience.
